Read the narration

A little team. Big possibilities.
Meet your little team of big ideas. Welcome to Lieuva's Agent Control Center.

Six specialists. Six superpowers.
Six specialists explore different corners: quality, experience, strategy, market, search, and three D. Each one brings back a report.

Special delivery: discoveries!
Think of every report as a little parcel of discoveries. One by one, the specialists deliver their findings to the Master.

The Master connects the dots.
The Master opens the parcels, connects the clues, and asks: what matters most today? The answer becomes up to three suggested tasks.

You get the deciding button.
Now, you are in charge. Review each task. Approve it, park it for later, or reject it. Approval saves your decision. Starting is a separate click.

Ready? Choose how the work happens.
Choose Execute for research or local code work. Choose Start manually when you will do the work yourself. The queue runs one agent job at a time.

Bring the result back home.
Read the result, and record what really happened. With automatic Master sync enabled, results and decisions return to the Master after the queue is finished.

One connected cycle. You in control.
Now the next plan can reflect your progress. Explore, decide, do, and learn. One connected cycle, with you in control.
