## 1. Brand decision

The customer-facing brand is **LIEUVA**.

- Do not reopen naming during WP4.
- Do not show AURA as a co-brand, transition lockup or “formerly AURA” label in normal product UI.

LIEUVA is broader than a virtual-art-gallery brand, but launch messaging must remain anchored in what the current product proves: image-based art and design work arranged in template-based immersive 3D spaces.

### Required authoritative trademark procedure

The owner must commission or document a professional clearance search before a broad public launch. A simple exact-name query is insufficient. For every register, save query strings, filters, export/screenshots, date, reviewer and the goods/services of each relevant result.

1. **EUIPO / TMview**
   - Open [EUIPO Search IP](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip) and [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/).
   - Search word marks `LIEUVA`, `LIEVA`, `LIUVA`, `LEUVA`, `LEEVA`, `LEEVIA`, `LIEV*`, `LI*VA` and phonetic/fuzzy equivalents.
   - Run exact, contains, begins-with and fuzzy searches; include live applications and registrations, EUIPO, Benelux/national offices and WIPO designations.
   - Review the full goods/services text and owners, not only Nice-class numbers.
2. **WIPO**
   - Open the [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database).
   - Repeat exact and fuzzy/phonetic variants, then filter International Registrations relevant to the EU, Benelux, UK, US and intended launch markets.
   - WIPO explicitly advises also checking national/regional registers; a WIPO-only search is not comprehensive.
3. **USPTO**
   - Open [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/) and follow the official [federal search method](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching).
   - Search the combined wordmark field for the same exact, stem and phonetic variants; inspect live records first without narrowing prematurely by class.
   - Review related software/SaaS, presentation, exhibition, hosting, design and publishing services. Also search US state registers and common-law web use if the US is a target market.
4. **BOIP / Benelux**
   - Open the official [BOIP Trademarks Register](https://www.boip.int/en/trademarks-register).
   - Search exact and strongly similar variants, including EU and international marks valid in Benelux. BOIP confirms the register includes Benelux, EU and international rights relevant there.
   - Run the Dutch Chamber of Commerce/Benelux trade-name check in addition to the trademark register.
5. **Counsel classification review**
   - Ask counsel to confirm the filing specification and territories. Likely areas to evaluate—not a filing instruction—are Nice 9 (software), 41 (cultural/educational presentation), 42 (SaaS/design/hosting), and only where the final service model requires them, 35 and 38.
   - Counsel must specifically assess the active LIEVA uses above for visual/phonetic similarity and related channels.

Do not claim “trademarked,” “registered” or “legally cleared” until counsel supplies a written result and any filing decision is documented.

## 3. Pronunciation

The fixed pronunciation is:

> IPA guidance: **/liˈuːvə/**

Use the same pronunciation in English, German and Dutch. Do not use a pseudo-French pronunciation. French speakers may naturally compress the first sounds; official founder, support, press and video speech still uses **lee-OO-vah**.

The written brand should accompany first spoken introductions where practical. ## 11. Beachhead audience

### Primary launch audience

**Independent visual artists and small galleries with a finished collection and a real exhibition, release or portfolio moment.**

### Secondary audience

**Managed graduate art/design-show teams at small schools or departments**, limited to a few hands-on pilots rather than a self-serve institutional promise.

### Deliberately not targeted at initial launch

- museums and large cultural institutions requiring procurement, DPA/SLA, accessibility governance or preservation commitments;
- architecture/interior firms requiring CAD/BIM/model import, client annotation and approval workflows;
- whole-university deployments and large cohort administration;
- enterprise brands requiring SSO, custom domains, analytics, high concurrency and service guarantees;
- social-world, multiplayer, commerce, native VR/app or arbitrary 3D-authoring users.

### Why this beachhead

It matches the current image/art metadata, gallery templates, wall placement and visitor experience. It also produces visually strong public proof and short decision cycles without pretending broader workflows already exist.

### Proof required before broader expansion

- 10–15 design-partner pilots with real deadlines;
- at least five consented, public, high-quality case studies;
- creators complete upload → arrange → preview → publish → update without intervention;
- visitors reliably enter and understand a Space on supported desktop/mobile devices;
- measured activation, engaged visits, sharing, support load and willingness to pay;
- one validated non-art prototype before architecture/brand messaging becomes a homepage promise.

## 12. Jobs-to-be-Done

**Core creator job:**

> When I have a finished body of work or collection to present, help me turn it into an intentional place people can enter and share, without making me learn 3D software or hire a specialist.

**Strongest pain point:** portfolios, feeds and folders flatten the work, while professional 3D tools and bespoke virtual production are too complex or expensive.

**Visitor job:** understand, navigate and share the work immediately in a browser, without an installation, mandatory account or game-control learning curve.

**Reason to choose LIEUVA:** curated templates, direct browser creation, spatial context, one shareable visitor link, guided/independent exploration and no required 3D expertise.

**Emotional outcome:** the creator feels the work has weight, context and a memorable destination rather than becoming another post in a feed.

## 16. Brand capitalization/style rules

- Customer-facing brand spelling is **LIEUVA**, always uppercase.
- `Lieuva`, `lieuva` and mixed-case stylizations are not permitted as a display wordmark or prose brand name.
- Lowercase is allowed only where the platform requires a machine identifier: `lieuva.com`, email addresses, URLs and social handles.
- Do not add an accent, separator, ligature or stylized punctuation to the textual brand.
- First meaningful mention on a surface: `LIEUVA`. Product contexts may say `LIEUVA Studio`; later mentions say `Studio`.
- Use sentence case for UI labels: `Guided tour`, `Focus view`, `Create a Space`.
- In ordinary prose, project/space/work are common nouns and lowercase. Capitalize `Project` or `Space` only in product labels, status headings, diagrams or when distinguishing the defined product entities.
- Voice: precise, calm, premium, creator-respecting and literal. Avoid metaverse, revolutionary, AI-powered, photorealistic, enterprise-ready or “any idea in 3D” claims unless independently proven.
- Creator identity leads inside published Spaces; LIEUVA attribution is restrained (`Made with LIEUVA`) and must not overpower the work.
