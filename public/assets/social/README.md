# Social preview assets

`lieuva-social-preview-v2.jpg` is the 1200 × 630, 149 KB default Open Graph image for the homepage, protected application routes, the Creator directory, and public Creator or Space pages that do not have their own cover.

Public Space cards and Creator cover images remain the first choice on their respective canonical URLs. The fallback filename is versioned because WhatsApp and other social clients cache preview media aggressively.

The image was generated for LIEUVA as a text-free architectural art-space background, then resized and JPEG-compressed for predictable social-card delivery. Keeping typography out of the bitmap prevents duplicated titles and preserves legibility across clients that crop previews differently.
